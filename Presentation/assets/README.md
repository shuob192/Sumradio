## radio-cover-illustration.png

- 用途: 表紙の背景。
- 作成方法: Codexの内蔵画像生成ツールで生成。
- 実際のデモ機材の写真ではなく、無線機と紙地図の概念イメージ。機種・性能・認証を示すものではない。
- スライドには「無線機はイメージ」と表示。

生成プロンプト:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: keynote cover background, 16:9 wide landscape 1536x864 or larger. Primary request: an elegant editorial photograph-style illustration of a generic unbranded handheld two-way radio used for disaster communication, on the right third of a dark navy desk. Long short-range walkie-talkie antenna, black rugged body, no recognizable logos, no lettering, no labels. A softly blurred topographic paper map under radio, subtle warm amber sidelight and cool navy blue fill. Left 60 percent is pure dark navy shadow with very subtle paper texture and ample negative space for separate editable Japanese slide text. Premium restrained presentation art, realistic materials, cinematic but not dramatic destruction. No people, no text, no infographic, no UI, no watermark, no disaster scene. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photo of the actual demo device. Keep radio contained in the right 38% and never clipped.

## task-map-illustration.png

- 用途: 4枚目の地図記号の説明。
- 作成方法: Codexの内蔵画像生成ツールで生成。
- 架空の地図。OpenStreetMapの地図、実アプリ画面、デモ用位置マスターの配置ではない。
- 水滴は給水要請、バツ印は通行障害の説明用。スライド上にも説明イメージである旨を表示。

生成プロンプト:

> Use case: productivity-visual. A clean fictional neighborhood map illustration for a presentation about disaster radio dispatch. Landscape 3:2. Warm off-white paper background, fine pale gray street grid, simple block buildings and small green park areas, a narrow pale blue river diagonally at the far right. Only two large location markers: a blue droplet inside a small white map pin near upper left, a burnt orange X inside a white map pin near lower right at a small bridge crossing the river. Top-down 2D restrained cartographic aesthetic, flat editorial map, understated high quality. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no screenshot frame, no legend, no scale bar. Fictional geography, not a real OpenStreetMap map. No additional marker icons. Markers large enough to read from distance, generous white negative space. Match a navy, warm ivory, blue and amber slide deck.
